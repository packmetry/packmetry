Operational Flexibility

The Three Box-Availability Modes

Packing constraints vary by situation. CartonLab calculates around your inventory realities.

Mode 01 shopping_cart

“Tell me what size to buy”

Ideal when you have no boxes yet. Synthesizes the optimal bounding container directly from your items to eliminate void waste.

Best for: New packaging purchases & store runs
Mode 02 inventory_2

“Use my boxes”

Pack everything strictly inside your current inventory. Calculates the best assignment across cartons already on hand.

Best for: Moving with saved boxes & stockroom limits
Mode 03 · Hybrid handshake

“Use what I have, buy the rest”

Hybrid allocation that fills on-hand boxes first to capacity, then calculates supplemental container sizes for leftovers.

Best for: Multi-room moves & overflow fulfillment

Documentation & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the 3D packing engine? expand_more
The engine utilizes heuristic 3D cuboid containment algorithms that model rotation constraints, stacking limits, and center-of-gravity balance. It accurately accounts for internal carton thickness (flute wall loss) rather than theoretical zero-thickness outer boundaries.
Can I use this for carrier dimensional rules (FedEx, UPS, DHL)? expand_more
Yes. The business tool lets you select standard carrier DIM divisors (e.g. 139 for domestic retail, 166, or metric 5000). CartonLab calculates billable dimensional weight alongside actual scale weight so you never encounter oversize surcharges unexpectedly.
Can I switch between metric (cm/kg) and imperial (in/lbs)? expand_more
Yes. The unit switcher in the navigation bar converts all active dimensions, tare allowances, and packaging tolerances seamlessly without losing calculation state.
